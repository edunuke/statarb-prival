Strategic Core Investment Thesis & Return Mechanics

Price divergences between historically paired equities are temporary, but unhedged market noise destroys profit. This system continuously identifies mispriced pairs, dynamically adjusts entry sensitivity, and limits risk through automated portfolio optimization.

Core Trading Engine

  • Long-short relative value trading on cointegrated S&P 500 stock pairs.
  • Dynamic hedge ratios calculated in real-time via Fast State Tracking.
  • Trades the normalized price spread divergence ($e_t$).
  • Automated profit-taking on mean reversion; hard stop-loss on structural breaks.

Adaptive AI Thresholds

  • Contextual machine learning dynamically selects entry sensitivity ($1.50\sigma$ to $2.25\sigma$).
  • Inputs include market volatility, VIX levels, and pair stability measures.
  • Business Impact: Relaxes hurdles during major dislocations to capture opportunity; tightens hurdles during calm regimes to prevent over-trading.

Institutional Risk Controls

  • Strict net-zero market beta target ($|\beta_{\text{net}}| \le 0.05$).
  • Style factor caps ($|F_{\text{momentum}}| \le 0.10$) prevent quant squeeze losses.
  • Single-stock NAV capped at $15\%$; gross portfolio leverage capped at $1.8\times$.
  • Order size capped at 1.5% of 20-day Average Daily Volume (ADV).

Governance Institutional Governance & Mathematical Control Layer

Risk Bounds

  • Net Beta: $|\beta_{\text{net}}| \le 0.05$ (market neutral).
  • Factor Limit: $|F_{\text{momentum}}| \le 0.10$ loading ceiling.
  • Sector Density: Max 3 active pair sleeves per sector.
  • Single Ticker NAV: Capped at 15% net exposure.

Liquidity & Execution

  • ADV Cap: Orders capped at 1.5% 20-day ADV.
  • Execution Model: Next-day Market-On-Open (T+1 lag).
  • Borrow Cost: 50 bps annual rate incorporated.
  • Quarantine: Exited pairs locked for 15 rebalance days.

Mathematical Control

  • Spread Calc: $e_t = (y_t - \alpha_t - \beta_t x_t) / \sqrt{F_t}$
  • CUSUM Test: $S_t = \sum v_k^2 / F_k \sim \chi^2(K)$
  • Frac Integration: GPH log-periodogram estimator.
  • Reward Equation: $R = 2.0 \cdot \text{Capture} \cdot e^{-0.3(\text{Days}/\text{HL}-1)}$

Executive Summary & Deployment Readiness

  • High-Conviction Selectivity: Executes institutional-grade pairs rather than deploying capital into low-quality noise.
  • Dynamic Protection: AI contextual thresholding tightens risk gates in choppy markets and expands exposure during clean dislocations.
  • Production Crash Resilience: Model state (Kalman states, context hyperplanes, quarantine timers) persists automatically to disk.
